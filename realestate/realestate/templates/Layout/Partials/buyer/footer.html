KeralaNest

KeralaNest is a web-based real estate platform designed to simplify property buying, selling, and management across Kerala. The system connects buyers, sellers, and administrators through a secure and user-friendly interface, making property transactions more transparent and efficient. The platform allows sellers to list properties such as land, houses, and apartments with complete details, images, and pricing information. Buyers can explore properties based on their preferences, view detailed descriptions, and contact sellers directly through the system. Administrators manage users, verify property listings, and ensure smooth platform operations..
